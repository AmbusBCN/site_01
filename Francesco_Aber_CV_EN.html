Profile

Art director and animation filmmaker (Centro Sperimentale di Cinematografia) with over ten years of creative direction for advertising, film and new media, based in Barcelona and working with clients in Spain, Italy and the US.

I design and lead animation programmes in schools: each project reaches around 500 students, is fully funded by the Italian Ministry of Culture, and ends in a short film screened in cinemas, including at the 80th Venice International Film Festival.

My graduation short The Age of Rust was selected at Annecy and shortlisted for the Nastri d’Argento. Today I bring generative AI and augmented reality into both my production and teaching work.
